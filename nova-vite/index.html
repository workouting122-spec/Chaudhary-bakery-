Before you reserve

The real questions.

Does reserving cost anything?

No. Reserving holds your place in the first production run. You only pay when it ships, and you can cancel any time before then.

Can I really repair it myself?

Yes. The screen and battery come out with one tool, and we sell the parts and guides at cost. Doing it yourself does not void your warranty.

How long will it get updates?

Six years of full OS updates and security patches, counted from the ship date, not the announcement.

What happens to my old phone?

Trade it in during checkout and we take the value off your first payment. If it still works, someone else gets it refurbished.
